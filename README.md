# ExposureMG's Xbox 360 Research

> [!CAUTION]
> If you are a clean-room engineer, **DO NOT** view hypervisor/, xenon-isa/sdk-docs/, or smc/. hypervisor/ contains a "full" 17559 hypervisor decompilation, xenon-isa/sdk-docs/ has information taken directly from the Xbox 360 SDK, and smc/ has decompilations of many Xbox 360 SMC firmwares.

## Contents

[xenon-isa]() - Documents on the Xbox 360 Xenon ISA: PPC64 2.02, AltiVec, and VMX128. Contains definitions, latencies, and more.

[protocols]() - Documents on various Xbox 360 protocols - XBDM, JRPC, JRPC2, XDRPC, and XRPC

[hypervisor]() - "Full" decompilation of the 17559 Hypervisor using Hax360's API.

[smc]() - Full decompilation of common SMC firmware types using Wurthless's smc360. 1 version per revision.
